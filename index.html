04 / POETRY

Where analytical thinking meets artistic voice

Blank Slate

Losing sooner losing faster At first your thoughts called you their master And lifelessly you shape the plaster That lines the coating of your grave The time once yours is now forgotten Once offered forth like fruit to kings Your health in locks, your youth a goner Just wait till old fat lady sings Remember when? You do? ..or don't you? Because it seems I haven't seen you since So keep on staring at the wall behind me And then complain about the pain life brings

A Place to Call

When my phone rings on a midnight drive And I check to see your name I don't think of the pain from past regrets Or the things I haven't done And the one that said, 'How could you?' Not when there's a time to call I pull over to the side To give you all of my attention I turn up the sound And plant my feet on the ground I'm ready to be with you
Inspired by Robert Frost's "A Time to Talk" — exploring the modern parallels of presence and connection
